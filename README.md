# Community-Based Experience and Services (COBES)
## *Shao Community Health Analytics Project*
## *By Nobilis Preambulus (UNILORIN MLS 24/25 Set)*
The Community-Based Experience and Services (COBES) program, initiated by the College of Health Sciences at the University of Ilorin, aims to immerse medical laboratory science students in real-world community health settings. This report presents the activities and findings from a COBES posting at Shao community, Kwara State, from March 17 to March 28, 2025, involving 68 final-year medical laboratory science students. The primary objective was to evaluate local health practices, identify gaps in healthcare services, and propose sustainable initiatives to address community health issues. Through structured questionnaires and direct engagement with community members, the students gathered valuable data despite encountering challenges such as language barriers and limited access to technology. The findings reveal a community with limited healthcare options, relying heavily on informal practitioners. Recommendations include enhancing healthcare education, improving service delivery, and fostering collaborations with local stakeholders. This experiential learning opportunity significantly contributed to students’ understanding of public health dynamics and reinforced the importance of community-based interventions in medical education. The outcomes underscore the need for continuous improvement of health services and the promotion of health awareness within the community.
